## The Opening Hook (canon — Book 3's "Prelude to Night Four")

Deep in the woods outside Bellemarsh — further in than the Hollow, past the old boundary line
and every patrol route the pack has ever walked — there is a tree that is wrong. Every so often
it flickers like a picture on a bad screen and goes faintly, coldly blue. Frogs have gone quiet
around it for weeks; the moss on its north side grows in a straight line.

At **3:33 AM**, on a moonless night with no reason to be special, the tree flickered and didn't
snap back. The bark bent outward like the surface of a pond and **Withered Bonnie** stepped
through:

- a tall rabbit shape, ears bent, old blue-purple fabric worn to threads;
- **one arm missing entirely**; the other a bare metal limb with wires hanging from the elbow;
- **no face** — a dark open hollow with two small points of light deep inside;
- moves clumsily, "as if it had forgotten how ground worked".

It turned its empty face slowly toward the town. The tree went back to being a tree.

Everything beyond this — what it is, where the tree leads, what it wants, whether anyone else
comes through — is open. See Open Questions.

## The Bellemarsh Record — seeds reserved for Books 4-5

From Book 3's end matter. In-world the Record is lost (hidden library room, last entry
31/12/1700, destroyed in the 2000 flood) and partly wrong; no character can read it. **Do not
explain these on the page until the author directs which belong to Book 4.**

- **Phantom Freddy** — burnt green bear, lower left leg missing, untouchable, "chaotically
  friendly", jump-scares faces. Told the log-keepers the Twisted had arrived. Other Phantoms
  unknown.
- **The Puppet(?)** — somewhere in a maze of corridors under the town, reached through the well
  in front of the Town Hall. Every **Christmas**, between midnight and six, it sings a song
  codenamed *Best Day Ever*, and four orbs of light come out of the woods and dance around the
  well.
- **Two sitting golden bears** (one gold, one more damaged) — danger 10, "DO NOT LOOK AT THEM",
  only ever glimpsed five times from the corner of the eye.
- **Jack-o Chica / Jack-o Bonnie** — Grim Foxy's burnt-out pack-mates; believed gone for good.
- The Record logs the Twisted as "Twisted Judges", danger 0-3.

Note for the author: Withered Bonnie is not in the Record. That fits a newcomer from somewhere
else (the blue tree), but it's the author's call.
